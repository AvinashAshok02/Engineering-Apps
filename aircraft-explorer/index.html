How aircraft work, from the wing up

Anatomy of Flight

Seven chapters on what keeps an airliner in the air: the aerodynamics of lift, the controls that steer it, the engines that push it, the structure and materials that hold it together, the systems that run it, and a whole flight from take-off to touchdown. Pick a chapter, then scroll.

Figures are representative round numbers for the class of aircraft or engine described, usually a 150–180 seat single-aisle airliner, not data for one specific model. The airfoil chapter uses an exact potential-flow solution (a Joukowski airfoil), which models attached flow; stall behaviour is drawn from typical wind-tunnel results.
