Where each engine works

No single engine covers the whole speed range. Turbines need the air slowed to subsonic speed at the compressor face; ramjets need the vehicle already moving fast; scramjets need it moving very fast. Solid bars are the normal operating range, faint bars are the stretch. Select a row to load that engine.

Temperatures, pressure ratios and speeds are representative round numbers for each class of engine, not data for one specific model. Turbine engines are shown at sea-level take-off, the ramjet at Mach 3 cruise and the scramjet at Mach 7. Pressure is given as a multiple of the air pressure ahead of the engine. Station numbers follow the usual gas-turbine convention (2 compressor face, 3 compressor exit, 4 turbine entry, 5 turbine exit, 9 nozzle exit).
